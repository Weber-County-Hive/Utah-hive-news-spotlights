A Memorial to Vietnam Veterans Exposed to Agent Orange Nears Completion in Sunset

A Syracuse Air Force veteran and his foundation are finishing a memorial at Sunset City Veterans Memorial Park, with an unveiling tentatively planned for Veterans Day.

Published Oct. 4, 2026 · Ran in the Sunday Buzz, Oct. 4, 2026
From local news reports

A memorial honoring Vietnam War veterans exposed to the herbicide Agent Orange is taking shape at Sunset City Veterans Memorial Park, behind Sunset City Hall. The effort is led by Larry Kerr of Syracuse, an Air Force veteran of the Vietnam War and founder of the Utah Agent Orange Veterans Foundation, a Davis County nonprofit whose stated mission is “to honor Veterans of the Vietnam war who were exposed to the deadly herbicide known as Agent Orange.”

Kerr first brought the idea to the Layton City Council in February 2025. The Sunset memorial’s black granite panels carry information about the Vietnam War and Agent Orange, KSL reported. FOX 13 reported that the panels were donated by the foundation and are being assembled by veterans.

“We’re just a bunch of old guys trying to get this done.”Larry Kerr, to KSL (Aug. 23, 2026)

Sunset Mayor Scott Wiggill has spoken in support of the memorial being part of the city’s veterans park, according to KSL.
