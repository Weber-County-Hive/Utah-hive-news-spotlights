Send us your story

Something happening in your town that your neighbors should know about? A memorial, a volunteer effort, a local milestone, or a news story we should share? Send it in.

webercountyhive@gmail.com

Put “News Spotlight” in the subject line. A few sentences in the body of the email, or a link to a news story, is all we need to start. We’ll write back to ask if you’d like to tell us more.

How spotlights are put together

  • Every spotlight says where it came from: sent in by a reader, or from local news reports.
  • Where something has been published about the story, the spotlight links to it.
  • When a reader sends a story, we write back to ask if they’d like to add more: background, photos, or anything else they want neighbors to know.
  • Facts and quotes are checked before a spotlight goes up.
  • Reader stories are checked before they go up. We don’t publish home addresses or private details.
  • New spotlights run in the Sunday Buzz, then stay here.
