# Utah Hive News Spotlights

Short spotlights on local news from around Utah, shared each week in the Sunday Buzz. Many are sent in by readers.
From Utah Hive Politics · from the publisher of The Weber County Hive.

Live site (after GitHub Pages is turned on): https://weber-county-hive.github.io/Utah-Hive-News-Spotlights/

## Files
- `index.html` — front page: cards, search, county filter, "Send us your story" box
- `spotlights-data.js` — the list of spotlights (add a new entry at the top for each new one)
- `style.css` — yellow, black and white styles shared by every page
- `sunset-agent-orange-memorial.html` — first spotlight (Oct. 4, 2026)

## Adding a spotlight
1. Copy an existing spotlight page, rename it, and change the text and sources.
2. Add an entry at the top of `SPOTLIGHTS` in `spotlights-data.js`.
3. Change `pageUpdated` in `spotlights-data.js`.

Reader stories: webercountyhive@gmail.com, subject "News Spotlight".
